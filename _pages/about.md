I am an **Associate Applied Machine Learning Specialist** at the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/) in Toronto, where I work on **model evaluation**, **Responsible AI**, **multimodal foundation models**, and **agentic systems**.

My research asks a practical question: how do we know whether large language models, vision-language models, and AI agents are reliable enough to deploy? That includes fairness and bias evaluation, auditability, explainability, and whether efficiency shortcuts change the conclusions of a benchmark. Recent work includes [HumaniBench](https://vectorinstitute.github.io/humanibench/) (ACM TIST), [VLDBench](https://vectorinstitute.github.io/VLDBench/) (Information Fusion), [AgentFinVQA](https://vectorinstitute.github.io/AgentFinVQA/) (NeurIPS 2026 workshop), and [FairSense-AgentiX](https://github.com/VectorInstitute/fairsense-agentix) (JOSS).

I serve as **Technical Lead** for Vector’s Interpretability in LLMs and Agents Bootcamp (~100 participants across 20 teams) and for the Fall Machine Learning Associates (MLA) program. I previously completed a Master of Engineering in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/) and a B.Tech (Honours) in Electronics and Communication Engineering at [IIIT Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/).
